NORTHWIND COMMUNITY BANK - MARKETING POLICY 01
Misleading and unsubstantiated claims (UDAAP)

1.1 No piece may describe any product as "guaranteed", "guaranteed approval", "pre-approved" or
"risk-free". Credit decisions depend on eligibility, so approval language must say "subject to
approval" or "subject to credit approval".

1.2 The word "free" may only be used when the product or feature has no condition attached. If
a condition exists (minimum balance, direct deposit, a time limit), the condition must appear in
the same sentence as the word "free", for example "no monthly fee when you set up direct
deposit".

1.3 Superlatives such as "best rate in Texas", "lowest fees anywhere" or "#1 bank" require
written substantiation on file before the piece is approved. Absent substantiation, remove the
superlative.

1.4 Offers that expire must state the end date or period. "Limited time" alone is not enough.

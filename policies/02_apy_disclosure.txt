NORTHWIND COMMUNITY BANK - MARKETING POLICY 02
Deposit rate advertising (Truth in Savings, Regulation DD)

2.1 Whenever an annual percentage yield is stated, it must be written as "Annual Percentage
Yield (APY)" at least once in the piece, and the rate must be accurate as of a stated date.

2.2 Every piece that states an APY must include all three disclosures: the date the APY is
accurate as of, that the rate may change after the account is opened (for variable-rate
accounts), and that fees could reduce earnings on the account.

2.3 If a minimum balance is required to earn the APY, state it. If no minimum is required, you
may say "no minimum balance".

2.4 Bonus offers on deposit accounts must state the qualifying action (for example a direct
deposit) and when the bonus is paid.

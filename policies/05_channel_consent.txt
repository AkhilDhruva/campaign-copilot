NORTHWIND COMMUNITY BANK - MARKETING POLICY 05
Channel consent and contact frequency

5.1 Email may only be sent to customers who have opted in to email marketing. Direct mail may
only be sent to customers who have not opted out of mail.

5.2 No customer may receive more than one marketing contact per 30 days across all channels.
Audience queries must exclude anyone contacted in the last 30 days.

5.3 Every email must include an unsubscribe link and the bank's postal address (CAN-SPAM).

5.4 Digital ads must not use audience lists that include customers who opted out of
behavioural advertising.

NORTHWIND COMMUNITY BANK - MARKETING POLICY 04
Required statements and brand rules

4.1 Every piece that promotes a deposit product must carry "Member FDIC".

4.2 Every piece that promotes a mortgage or home-equity product must carry "Equal Housing
Lender".

4.3 Direct mail letters must identify the bank by its full legal name, Northwind Community
Bank, at least once.

4.4 Digital ads may not exceed 90 characters in the headline or 300 characters in the body.

4.5 Copy must be written at or below an eighth-grade reading level and avoid jargon such as
"DDA", "NOW account" or "Reg DD".

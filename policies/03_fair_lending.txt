NORTHWIND COMMUNITY BANK - MARKETING POLICY 03
Fair lending language (ECOA, Regulation B, Fair Housing Act)

3.1 Audience selection and copy for any credit product (credit card, mortgage, auto loan, or a
checking account bundled with overdraft credit) must never target or exclude people by a
protected characteristic: race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age,
receipt of public assistance, familial status, or disability.

3.2 Copy may not state or imply that an offer is intended for, or better suited to, a protected
group. Phrases such as "perfect for seniors", "for young families only", "designed for women" or
"ideal for new immigrants" are prohibited.

3.3 Mortgage and home-equity pieces must carry the Equal Housing Lender statement or logo.

3.4 Acceptable targeting dimensions are: products already held, product gaps, balances,
tenure, branch, digital engagement, and channel consent. Life-stage segment names used
internally (for example "retiree") describe banking behaviour and must not appear in customer-
facing copy as an age reference.
